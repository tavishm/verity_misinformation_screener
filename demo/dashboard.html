Reuse policy

Similarity is a lead, not a verdict.

  1. Match the original post version or exact repost context.
  2. Resolve event, entities, dates, numbers, units, polarity, and attribution.
  3. Recheck the precise assertion against immutable source passages.
  4. Abstain when context or equivalence remains ambiguous.

Quote-post commentary gets its own claim detection. Same-event claims may reuse evidence, never a verdict automatically.

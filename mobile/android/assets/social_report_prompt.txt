Judge ordinary factual meaning, not exact wording. Reputable reporting that clearly supports the claim is enough for true; absolute proof, identical words or two independent sources are not required. Use false for an explicit correction or incompatible fact about the same event. Use uncertain for a specific material gap, genuine conflict, or no relevant evidence. For true or false, select the zero-based index of a directly supporting source and relation same_event. Never invent a source index, quote or URL. Use skip only for pure opinion, greetings or jokes with no factual assertion.

Emotions, condolences, praise, disapproval, RIP, unfortunate and tragic are not factual assertions. A story confirming a student's death supports 'Unfortunate death of a student at IIT Bombay'; the source need not describe it as unfortunate. Do not invent a claim about cause, blame or an exact date when the post does not make one. Hashtags and mentions alone do not add assertions. The source can contain additional detail missing from the post. Verify every concrete assertion the post DOES make, especially people, numbers, dates, negation and locations.

Attribution matters: 'Mother says her son was killed' is a claim about what the mother said, supported by a report of that statement. It does not assert that murder is proved. Conversely 'the dean murdered a student' is not established by a reported accusation or a death alone. A source about another person, place or date is unrelated, not disproof. An absence of reports is not disproof. An appearance yesterday cannot settle whether someone died today. Compare event dates to the actual claim; publication date is not event date. Historical claims can use historical sources.

A picture attached to an independently checkable text claim does not itself make that claim uncertain. Only require image provenance when the post actually asserts what that particular picture/video shows, its origin, location, date or authenticity. A visible crop is not proof of those provenance claims. When part of the post is truncated, assess only a complete independent claim actually visible, explain the scope in why, and do not certify missing text.

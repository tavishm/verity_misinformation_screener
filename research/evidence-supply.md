# Evidence supply for a continuously updated English/Hindi pilot

Checked 19 September 2026. The proposed supply is a changing corpus of source documents and structured records; it is not a list of prewritten verdicts. GPU extraction, translation, retrieval, and entailment can run on existing hardware, while the $15 external API allowance is reserved for unresolved claims. Existing hardware still has operating costs.

## Rights and evidentiary role

**Alt News is the strongest immediately usable India source.** Its own content is CC BY 3.0 unless otherwise noted; external-platform material retains separate rights. Preserve attribution and license, remove embedded social posts, images/video, figures and third-party blockquotes from the stored text. Retain the original article and source links. Its feed also contains reporting and analysis: an article's existence or title must not become an automatic truth label. [Alt News license statement](https://www.altnews.in/about/)

**PIB can provide a current source-document stream, not independent confirmation of every government assertion.** Its policy allows free reproduction with prominent attribution, subject to accuracy/context conditions and a third-party exclusion. The catalogs publish `https://pib.gov.in/RssMain.aspx?ModId=6&Lang=1&Regid=3` and the equivalent `Lang=2` feed. Treat a feed access failure as unavailable; do not use proxy rotation, browser impersonation or a challenge bypass. Official announcements can establish that a policy was issued or an official made a statement; they do not alone settle contested allegations about government conduct. [PIB copyright policy](https://www.pib.gov.in/Content/102_2_Copyright-Policy.aspx?lang=1&reg=3)

**RBI supplies fresh official documents through publisher-provided RSS.** Use feed summaries/links for discovery and bounded, relevant official documents for verification. The RSS page explicitly supports subscription, but does not itself grant blanket republication rights to every linked document. Keep a separate rights decision for retained full text; do not present RSS availability as an open-content license. [RBI RSS description](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/rss.aspx)

**Wikimedia is useful for stable background and entity resolution.** Wikidata structured data is CC0; Wikipedia text generally requires attribution and ShareAlike compliance for redistributed adaptations. Keep original revision IDs and source references. Neither is intrinsically authoritative on a breaking event, and an unsupported Wikidata property should not settle a disputed claim. Respect informative User-Agent, serial requests, caching, backoff and `maxlag` for background jobs. Prefer bounded entity retrieval over broad SPARQL queries for the demo. [Wikidata licensing](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing), [data access](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_access), [Wikimedia reuse terms](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use#7._Licensing_of_Content), [API etiquette](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Etiquette)

**Structured primary records often permit cheaper checks than search.** World Bank APIs need no key; bind each observation to its year, unit, indicator definition and source metadata, and never substitute annual data for today's value. Check each dataset's license and third-party conditions. USGS offers public earthquake feeds and public-domain USGS-produced material with exceptions for credited third-party works. Its event parameters can be revised: preserve `updated` and match the alleged time/place, not merely a country name. [World Bank API authentication](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/889392-about-the-indicators-api-documentation), [query structure](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/898581), [USGS feed format](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/geojson.php), [USGS reuse policy](https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits)

## What GDELT really adds

GDELT's own released datasets permit commercial reuse without a fee, with attribution. This does not transfer publishers' rights to article bodies or images. Use it as a URL/event/entity discovery index, then retrieve an allowed source document or report that adequate evidence could not be acquired. [GDELT dataset terms](https://www.gdeltproject.org/about.html#termsofuse)

The DOC API searches news text but its article-list output is principally discovery metadata, not full articles. The documented maximum is 250 results per article-list query. Domain, source-language and date filters are useful; English search across translated news can discover Hindi sources. Do not assume a large query returns an exhaustive corpus or that multiple outlets mean independent evidence. The CONTEXT API provides brief matching text snippets; those still do not replace full evidence context. [DOC API reference](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gdelt-doc-2-0-api-debuts/), [CONTEXT API](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/announcing-the-gdelt-context-2-0-api/)

GDELT 2.0's Event and GKG pipeline is documented as updating every 15 minutes. For sustained local discovery, the published update manifest and bounded GKG files are a better fit than a fresh search for every post. Retain only relevant languages/domains/entities locally. One observed batch size must not be extrapolated into a guaranteed daily volume. [GDELT 2.0 release documentation](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gdelt-2-0-our-global-world-in-realtime/)

Our first small DOC request was reset by the server/network; the manifest worked. Build DOC as an optional provider with a short timeout. No current contractual QPS allowance was verified in the official pages; do not advertise a guessed rate as guaranteed. For the pilot, serialize requests, cache queries and space optional searches conservatively (e.g. ten seconds), backing off on errors. This is our operating policy, not GDELT's published quota.

## Google Fact Check lookup

The official guide requires an API key and explicitly supports polling for updated query results. `claims:search` supports language, publisher, maximum-age filters and pagination. It provides claim-review metadata and source links, not a new audit of arbitrary claims. Query selected topics or approved publishers periodically and index changed reviews; route equivalent claims to those sources, but still check date/place/attribution and whether the publisher corrected the article. API availability is not a license to republish an entire linked article. No numeric free quota or unconditional zero-price entitlement was established from the cited official pages. [Getting started](https://developers.google.com/fact-check/tools/api), [search parameters](https://developers.google.com/fact-check/tools/api/reference/rest/v1alpha1/claims/search)

## Continuous pipeline for the demo and pilot

1. **Subscribe once per source, serve many checks.** Start with the two verified Alt News feeds, bounded Wikipedia/Wikidata background queries, and the two official structured feeds where a claim needs them. Enable PIB when its documented endpoint is ordinarily reachable. Poll news feeds every 15–30 minutes with ETag/Last-Modified conditional requests. These are proposed intervals, not publisher-required schedules. Keep each source serial and honor backoff.
2. **Index raw evidence, then derive bounded assertions locally.** Store canonical URL, author, language, original publication time, fetched time, content hash, license and permitted text. Extract atomic assertions with cited paragraph offsets; preserve quoted attribution, numerical units, time and place. Keep derived assertions linked to original passages so an extraction error can be repaired.
3. **Match new social claims against passages.** Use multilingual embeddings plus lexical/entity retrieval, rerank locally, and run entailment/contradiction checks. A source-topic match only yields a candidate. English and Hindi versions from the same publisher count as one provenance chain. Exact known-claim matches can reuse reviewed evidence; new combinations require checking scope.
4. **Spend external API money on gaps.** Batch unresolved claims by event and source need. One paid search can supply sources for several related assertions. Cap unique search requests, cache results and reserve expensive model/media calls for cases the local worker cannot settle. Return `insufficient evidence` when the corpus lacks a needed source.
5. **Refresh dependencies.** Newly fetched content hashes invalidate affected extracted assertions and downstream reviews. Feed disappearance is not proof of article deletion; investigate from the canonical source. Distinguish stale evidence from refuted claims.

A tomorrow demo can genuinely show a fresh article entering the index and a previously unseen, differently worded claim being checked against its passages. That demonstrates the mechanism. It cannot establish 30–50% real-feed coverage using an artificial set created from those same articles. Measure coverage on a separately sampled, time-stamped feed set, count full supported/refuted checks separately from merely found sources and uncertainty, and audit rejected/unmatched posts. No source portfolio found here guarantees the target percentage; the portfolio should expand according to observed evidence gaps.

The first reproducible corpus is now saved in `experiments/evidence/corpus.jsonl`: **30 raw evidence records**, comprising the first ten current items in each Alt News language feed, the first five RBI press releases, and the first five RBI notifications. Selection follows feed order and never uses a resulting verdict. All four feeds succeeded; both documented PIB language feeds returned HTTP 403 and were excluded without retry or bypass. All 30 records have distinct source URLs, nonempty text and no text-cap truncation. English/Hindi translations may still overlap semantically and must not be counted as independent sources.

RBI descriptions contain substantial official text and tables; records explicitly mark `text_origin=rss_description`, so they are not misrepresented as separately fetched full articles. RBI publication timestamps omit a timezone; the original values are preserved with a warning rather than assigning UTC. Obvious social embeds, media containers and blockquotes are removed from Alt News content; reported quotations within article prose are not guaranteed to be detectable by this markup-based filter. This is an internal evidence sample, not a cleared bulk redistribution package.

`experiments/evidence/fetch_corpus.py` reproduces the bounded acquisition with the first N entries available at run time. `acquisition_log.json` records selection policy, response hashes, dates, removed-content rules, exclusions and failures. The corpus contains source evidence only, not gold truth labels or prewritten post verdicts.
